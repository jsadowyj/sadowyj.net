## about

First off, I know what you're thinking -- How do I pronounce my last name? The IPA pronunciation is "sədɔvɪj" but I tell everybody it's "sado-vee" because that seems to roll off the tongue easier. 

I come from a family of eastern-european immigrants. Half of my family is Romanian 🇷🇴, and the other half is Ukrainian 🇺🇦. I spent most of my life around the Romanian side, which gave me an ear for the language, but almost no usable vocabulary, so in January 2025 I started learning it properly.

### education

I graduated with an Associates of Applied Science in Computer Science before graduating high-school, and finished my Computer Science degree at Western Governors University in December 2025.

### language learning

Romanian is the one I've put the most into. Starting from zero, I'm past 16,000 known words in [LingQ](https://www.lingq.com/en/) and somewhere around upper intermediate. Russian is what I'm working through now, on the same routine. Beyond that I'll pick up random phrases in whatever crosses my path; usually chasing the pronunciation more than the vocabulary. There's a specific satisfaction for me in landing a sound English doesn't have.

### homelab

Three Intel NUCs running Talos Linux as a Kubernetes cluster, with every manifest in git and ArgoCD reconciling it. The network is all Unifi (written in terraform too, so VLANs and firewall policy are all declarative).

### cooking

My newest hobby and the one I'm worst at. The format that finally stuck is one prep day, then a week of dinners that need no decisions. The recipes are plain-text [Cooklang](https://cooklang.org) files in git, and I use the `cook` cli to turn each week's menu into a shopping list to minimize waste.
